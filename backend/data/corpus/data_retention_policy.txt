Data Retention Policy v2.1

User request logs are retained for 90 days in hot storage (S3 Standard) and moved to cold storage (S3 Glacier) for 3 years before permanent deletion.

Model inference outputs are not stored beyond the request lifecycle unless the user explicitly enables receipt logging via the dashboard setting "Save Receipts".

Personal data subject to GDPR right-to-erasure requests must be purged within 72 hours of a verified deletion request. Contact privacy@aiflow.dev to initiate.

Audit logs for billing and compliance are retained for 7 years in accordance with financial regulations. These logs do not contain request content, only metadata.

Database backups are retained for 30 days with point-in-time recovery up to 5 minutes granularity.
